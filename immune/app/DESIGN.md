# Immune console design brief

SoulStack brings rules, skills and immune cells to the agents a person already uses. Its immune console turns the machine's plain files into a legible account of what needs a human and what has been made safe.

**One idea:** lift the immunity layer, from the first report to the guard that remembers it.

Quiet, alive, direct: the brief asks for “one sentence”, “plain words” and “SoulStack's worlds”. A developer opens Overview to decide what needs them, then moves to one record, not a wall of counters. The six-step record line is the visual signature: found, reviewed, fixed, fix reviewed, landed, guarded. Evidence stays below it; no decorative charts or invented confidence scores.

## Inventory

- Overview: one verdict, fixed/guarded summary, primary action, the record flow from new report to guarded, human queue and recently landed work.
- Triage: one record, its evidence and human decision controls, with keyboard help.
- Records: human-first groups and four quiet columns; record detail keeps the full history.
- Machine: cycles, commits and the records they closed, guards, antigen floor/latest, a read-only GitHub issues snapshot, shared CLI board.
- Health: current verdict, platform/check grid, failing checks, holes, recent run strip.
- Runs: expandable retained results.
- Cells: grown and stem cells.
- Findings and Explorations: readable notes.

Overview owns the five patrol views as secondary navigation. Machine owns its six tabs. A demo shows a sample-project banner on every view, so nobody reads its records as a real product's bugs. Hash routes survive refresh and static hosting. All views use the same spacing, controls and type scale.

## Worlds and evidence

Recursive and the six existing SoulStack palettes are reused locally, without network fonts. Undertow is precise, Coffee warm, Water light, Crimson vivid; Empryo and proxySoul remain selectable. Day/night, world and motion choices are remembered. Scenery stays at the edge: the stacked mark and a live dot, never behind evidence. Reduced motion stops transitions.

The local app reads actual records and run artifacts. The exported showcase explicitly identifies a fictional product, Lantern, and disables all writes. Demo records are original, with two cycles and all six folders represented. No Empryo records, paths or titles are copied.

## Proof plan

Three hypotheses: every route and theme is reachable without layout collisions; human decisions preserve one record and its history while rejecting unsafe requests; CLI and app board counts are identical. Scope is the local Node HTTP and browser front doors on this host. Use temporary copies for decisions, a 15-minute browser budget and per-command deadlines. Save all views at 390/1440 in day/night plus all-world layout measurements. Do not mutate the reference console.
