# Checking a visual change

A visual change is done only after you have looked at it.

1. `bun run dev`, then open `http://localhost:5190/?world=<id>&mode=<mode>`.
2. Look at every section in at least: Undertow night, Coffee day, Crimson night, Water day. Before a
   release, all twelve.
3. At 390 (phone), 1440 (laptop) and 3440 (ultra-wide) wide.
4. Check: nothing sticks out of its card, no text under the top bar, the matrix is readable on a
   phone, the terminal output fits, focus rings are visible, and `?motion=still` is calm.
5. Report with the screenshots, one line each.
