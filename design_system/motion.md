# Motion

Motion answers the visitor or draws the eye once. Nothing loops in the reading area except the immune
loop's single dot.

| Moment | How | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Painting | Soft pigment blooms drifting at the page edges (`scene.ts`), faint behind text, paused when the tab is hidden | The site is alive without competing with the words |
| World or mode switch | A circular ink bloom from the click point (view transition) | The change feels like Empryo, not a flash |
| Hero terminal | Lines of setup output appear one by one when the terminal scrolls into view | Shows the product working |
| Layer choice | The chosen layer straightens and slides; the panel fades up 6px | Shows what changed |
| Immune loop | One dot runs the ring in 9 seconds | The loop is the idea; one moving thing is enough |
| Hover | Cards lift 2px; nothing scales except the world dots | Feedback, not fireworks |

## Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion` or `?motion=still` sets `data-motion="still"`: the painting is drawn once,
the terminal prints at once, the loop dot is hidden and world switches are instant.
