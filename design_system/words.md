# Words

- Plain words, sentence case, short. One line under each visual.
- Say what the visitor gets, not how it is built: "Your text stays", not "idempotent marker blocks".
- Name things the way the product names them: `soulstack update`, `tcell-helper`, "rules", "skills",
  "agents", "presets", "setup".
- Paths are the user's defaults: "in your home folder by default".
- No jargon, no hype, no long dashes, no invented numbers or quotes.
- Headlines state the benefit: "Lands in every agent you use", "Stays current with one word".
