# Identity

## The one idea

**A stack you can lift layer by layer.** SoulStack is six layers (rules, skills, agents, immunity, presets,
setup) that land in whichever agents a person already uses. Every screen shows a layer, where it
lands, or how it stays current. The visitor should understand the whole stack without reading a
paragraph.

## Where it comes from

- **Empryo's family.** Same six proxySoul worlds, same Recursive typeface with a per-world voice, same
  living painting at the edges, same Mote. A visitor from empryo.com should feel at home at once.
- **The owner's vibe.** Short, direct, excited ("amazing", "stunning", "not vibed"), visual over
  prose, allergic to jargon and templates. The site answers with visuals plus one short line.
- **Terminal honesty.** The product is a script, so the hero shows its real output, not a drawing of
  an app.

## The signature moments

1. The hero terminal typing setup's real output beside a living Mote.
2. The stack: six layers that tilt forward when chosen and open into what they put where.
3. The immune loop: one dot running explore → disprove → make permanent → patrol, with `tcell-helper` at the
   centre.
4. The world switch: an ink bloom from the click point that repaints everything, logos included.

## Never

- A SaaS card kit with identical shadows, gradient washes, or all-caps eyebrows over every heading.
- Numbered markers on things that are not a sequence.
- Screenshots of private windows or real sessions.
- Paths presented as if they were ours: every path on the page is a default in the user's home.
- Placeholders, "coming soon", invented quotes or numbers.
